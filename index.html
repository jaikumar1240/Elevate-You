FAQs

Common questions.

Why is there no free session?

Free slots attract people who aren't committed — that wastes both our time. A small ₹89 payment ensures every student I work with is serious, which means I can focus entirely on giving you my best feedback.

Which plan should I start with?

Start with the ₹89 Diagnosis Session. It's designed to identify your exact problem. At the end of that call, I'll tell you whether a resume review, mock interview, or a pack makes more sense for you — no guessing.

How do I book sessions other than the ₹89 one?

Exactly the same way — click the booking button on the plan you want and pay securely via Razorpay. Right after payment you'll be able to pick a time slot, and you'll get a confirmation by email. Most sessions happen within 24–48 hours of booking.

I'm in 2nd year — is it too early?

Not at all. Building your resume and communication skills early gives you a massive advantage. Many students only start when placement season hits — by then it's rushed. Starting now means you can prepare properly.

What happens in the 15-minute Diagnosis Session?

I ask you 3–4 targeted questions about your situation — what you're applying for, what's happened so far, and what you think the issue is. Then I tell you your #1 blocker and one specific thing you can do immediately. You also get session notes after the call.
